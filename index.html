About

Healing out loud

After surviving a stroke at 18 and spending months misdiagnosed before learning she has lupus, Lauren started sharing what actually helps her feel like herself again.

Her page mixes honest health talk, everyday Arizona style and life with her two bullies and their puppies. She wants every woman who watches to feel empowered, understood and like life gets good.
